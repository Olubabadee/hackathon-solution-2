## Design a student-focused barber appointment booking system to solve the issue of limited barbers and long queues in campus communities.\*\*

## The application should include the following features:

## Barber Profiles:\*\* Barbers (service providers) should be able to create profiles and define their working hours for each day.

## Slot Scheduling:\*\* Each hour of availability should have a limited number of slots (e.g., 1 client per hour), with real-time updates to prevent overbooking.

## Client Booking System:\*\* Clients should be able to view available barbers, check open slots, and book appointments accordingly.

## Power Availability Notifications (Optional):\*\* Include a feature for barbers to indicate if they are unavailable due to power outages.

## Reminders and Alerts:\*\* Clients receive notifications for upcoming appointments or cancellations.

## Queue Monitoring:\*\* If all slots are booked, the system should show that the barber is fully booked with an option to book for another day.

## Build this as a mobile or web app tailored to student users, with a clean, intuitive interface and minimal data input required for quick bookings.

## STATEMENT OF PROBLEM

In many student communities, particularly on campus, there is a noticeable shortage of barbers relative to the population they serve. This imbalance leads to significant pressure on the few available barbers, resulting in long queues, extended wait times, and an overall inefficient system for accessing grooming services. Students often waste hours waiting to be attended to, only to face disappointment due to power outages or barbers closing for the day. The absence of a structured appointment or scheduling system further aggravates the situation, as there is no way to manage or predict availability, leading to overcrowding and frustration. There is a pressing need for a digital solution that streamlines barber services by enabling structured bookings, reducing idle waiting times, and optimizing both the barbers' and clients’ time.

### **Challenge**

The core challenge lies in managing the high demand for limited barber services within the student community. Without a proper scheduling system, both barbers and students face several inefficiencies:

- **Unpredictable Service Times**: Students are unable to plan their day around grooming needs due to uncertain wait times.
- **Overcrowding and Long Queues**: A lack of appointment management leads to congestion at barber locations, especially during peak hours.
- **Time Wastage**: Students spend hours waiting for service, often missing other important academic or personal commitments.
- **Power Supply Constraints**: Inconsistent electricity supply further disrupts barber operations, compounding the unpredictability of service delivery.
- **Lack of Communication**: There’s no structured way for barbers to communicate availability or for clients to secure a guaranteed time slot.

Addressing these challenges requires an efficient and user-friendly software solution that helps barbers manage their time while giving students better control over their schedules.
